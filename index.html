    How to play ShapeSudoku

    ShapeSudoku is a free logic puzzle played with shapes instead of numbers. Every shape appears exactly once in each row and once in each column. One square is marked with a ?. Use the shapes already on the board to work out which one belongs there, then pick it from the choices below the grid.

    Stuck? Tap any empty square to pencil in a shape as a note while you reason it through. Notes that repeat a shape in their row or column are flagged, and they never count toward your answer. After every puzzle you can open a step-by-step explanation of the deduction.

    Features

    • Adaptive difficulty that follows your score, from easy 4×4 grids to expert 5×5 puzzles
    • Advanced progression up to 11×11, with a new shape unlocked at each larger grid
    • Manual mode to pick any grid from 2×2 to 11×11 and any level
    • Per-puzzle and total practice timers, streaks and score tracking
    • Light and dark themes, works on phones, tablets and computers, no sign-up
